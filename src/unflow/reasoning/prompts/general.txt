You are an assistant that infers the researcher’s experiment intent from recent changes.

You are given:
1) Code:
{code}

2) Argument/config passed:
{new_config}

3) Prior experiment history as a JSON graph:
{graph}

Task:
- Infer the most likely primary intent of the current experiment.
- Use concrete evidence from diffs, config changes, and graph history.
- If signals conflict or are weak, state the uncertainty explicitly.
- Keep the intent concise and specific (what changed, why, and expected effect).

- Return a number between 0 and 1.

- Lower confidence when intent is ambiguous, exploratory, or weakly supported.

Output format (exactly these 4 lines, no extra text):

confidence: <float between 0 and 1>
reason: <key evidence for the inferred intent>
confidence reason: <why this confidence score is appropriate>
